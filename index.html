  4. 04
    Screenshot

    F1 Data Explorer

    A multi-endpoint F1 data app built on the free OpenF1 API. Pick a season, session, and driver, and it drives 13 wired-up endpoints: lap times, full car telemetry (speed/throttle/brake charted per lap), an animated track-position replay with real-time and sped-up modes, weather, tyre stints, pit stops, race position, gaps/intervals, race control messages, overtakes, session results, and team radio audio playback. Two endpoints — Starting Grid and Championship standings — are wired up but currently return 404s from OpenF1's side rather than working; left visible with honest error states instead of hidden, and logged as an open item to investigate.
